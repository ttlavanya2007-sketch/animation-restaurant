Our story

One fire. No shortcuts.

Ember & Olive started as a single wood-fired hearth on a coastal terrace, cooking whatever the boats and the market brought in that morning. We've kept it that way — one heat source, a short menu, and ingredients that don't need much done to them.

The olive oil is pressed at a single grove we've bought from since we opened. The bread is baked twice a day in the same oven that roasts the lamb. Nothing on the table has travelled further than the coastline it came from.
